# Privacy Policy

## General

Jonah Y-M (@TG-Techie) built this app as a free utility app.

This service is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors that the EnumURL app does not collect any data.
There are no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no third-party SDKs.
Your pagelets and their state are stored only on your device (and in your device backups, which you control).
Web pages you add are loaded directly by your device using Apple's WebKit; those sites see your requests just as they would in any browser, and their own privacy policies apply to them.
As the publisher/owner of the app, Jonah has access to Apple's general developer statistics.
